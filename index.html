📊 How to Read the Stats Like a Pro

Read these 4 simple tips before you start picking players!

🏏

1. Total Runs vs Batting Average

Total Runs is just how many runs scored in all games. But Average tells you how many runs they score each time they bat! A high total might just mean they played lots of games.

⚡

2. Strike Rate (Speed)

Runs scored per 100 balls. Strike rate 130+ is fast and dangerous! Strike rate 105 means slow scoring, which puts pressure on other batters.

🎯

3. Bowling Economy (Thriftiness)

Average runs given per 6-ball over. An economy of under 6.5 is super stingy! An economy of 8.5+ is expensive and leaks boundaries.

⚠️

4. Matches Played (Sample Size Trap!)

Beware! A player who scored 85 in only 2 matches has a huge average, but can you trust them in a tournament? 10+ matches proves real reliability!

About CricTeam XI

Roll No: 23B3006 | ET 617 End-Sem

CricTeam XI is an instructional application designed for collaborative problem-solving on shared tablets.

Card Alignment

  • P6 (Collaborative Problem Solving): Supports shared artefact (Team Sheet), explicit reasoning, peer feedback reviews, and structured resolution of disagreements.
  • T10 (Sports Team Selection): Real, curated cricket statistics challenging statistical misconceptions (totals bias M1, sample size outlier M2, and role balance).
  • L2 (First-Gen Urban Learner): Pass-and-play turn taking for shared tablets in NGO after-school centers; simple conversational English; low bandwidth; no login required.
  • C5 (Teacher-in-the-Loop): PIN-protected supervision dashboard allowing facilitators to audit collaboration equity and Approve or Override rule-based AI feedback messages.

Design Challenge Answer

The system tracks individual actions, reasons, and peer reviews per partner. If one partner's share exceeds 70% or one has 0 reasons, the app visibly flags the imbalance, prompts the quieter partner by name, gates final submission, and notifies the facilitator.
