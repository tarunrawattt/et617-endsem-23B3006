Coach’s Briefing

How to Read Cricket Stats Like a Pro

Four quick principles to spot solid players and avoid misleading numbers.

1. Total Runs vs Batting Average

Total Runs shows quantity. Average reveals reliability per match. A high total often just reflects playing many games without assessing consistency.

2. Strike Rate (Scoring Speed)

Runs scored per 100 balls. Strike rate 125+ keeps the scoreboard moving. Strike rate 105 slows down the team and exerts pressure on partners.

3. Bowling Economy (Tightness)

Runs conceded per 6-ball over. Economy under 6.5 dries up scoring. An economy of 8.5+ is expensive and leaks boundaries.

4. Matches Played (Sample Trap)

A batter with an 85 average in only 2 matches may have had one lucky afternoon. Prioritise proven form across 10+ matches!

About CricTeam XI

Candidate ID: 23B3006 • ET 617 End-Sem

CricTeam XI is an instructional system designed for collaborative mathematical problem-solving on shared tablets in low-resource community learning centers.

Card Alignment & Pedagogy

  • P6 (Collaborative Problem Solving): Mediated pass-and-play turns, shared XI sheet artefact, mandatory peer justifications, and structured disagreement resolution.
  • T10 (Sports Team Statistics): Real fictional cricket stats crafted to expose M1 (aggregate totals bias) and M2 (small sample size bias).
  • L2 (First-Generation Urban Learner): Designed for shared 10" tablets in after-school NGO centers; clear Class 7–8 English; 100% offline-ready in localStorage.
  • C5 (Teacher-in-the-Loop): Facilitator dashboard via PIN 1234 to audit equity and approve or override rule-based AI advice.

Collaboration Anti-Monopoly Response

Dynamic participation tracking alerts learners if one partner monopolizes > 70% of actions, addresses the quiet partner by name, and blocks team finalization until both contribute.
